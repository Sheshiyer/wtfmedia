## Evidence classification

This checklist keeps four kinds of evidence separate:

| Marker | Meaning | Can satisfy a staging or production gate? |
|---|---|---|
| `VERIFIED-SOURCE` | Reproducible from this checkout with Git or local commands | No, unless the gate is explicitly source-only |
| `REPORTED` | Supplied in a handoff or conversation without a host-visible receipt | No |
| `STAGING-ACCEPTED` | Exact deployed revision, environment receipts, and real authenticated IAB observations | Only the named staging gate |
| `PRODUCTION-ACCEPTED` | Owner-authorized production evidence for the exact deployed revision | Only the named production gate |

## Explicitly deferred from the Beta 0.1 exit decision

These remain work items, not hidden promises or evidence gaps that may be
silently inferred from the Beta release:

- **BETA-DEFER-01 — provider and YouTube persistence:** AI Route and YouTube
  Analytics remain non-persisted local previews until a separately approved
  provider integration, data contract, and lifecycle exists.
- **BETA-DEFER-02 — scheduled jobs:** YouTube/provider refresh, reporting, and
  other scheduled jobs require separately approved source access, schedule,
  failure handling, and operational receipts.
- **BETA-DEFER-03 — long-context compaction:** no claim of automatic
  compaction, durable full-session continuity, or context-checkpoint recovery
  is allowed until paging, checkpoint, failure, and evaluation criteria pass.
- **BETA-DEFER-04 — full Alpha alignment:** the release may verify the
  explicitly listed authenticated Beta routes and viewports, but it does not
  certify a wholesale merge, complete visual/interaction parity, or all future
  Alpha capability adoption.

## Handoff rule

Future agents must append evidence to the applicable checklist row rather than
upgrading a `VERIFIED-SOURCE` or `REPORTED` fact into `STAGING-ACCEPTED` or
`PRODUCTION-ACCEPTED`. A failed, unknown, missing, or mismatched exact-revision
receipt blocks the corresponding release decision.
